## What the gap analysis actually says

1. **Savo's deficit is 100% data, not architecture.** Every dimension
   where Savo scores below competitors (proof, clients, testimonials,
   reviews, certifications, timeline, team) is blocked on verified
   input, not on engineering or design. The components exist and render
   the moment data arrives.
2. **Competitors' strengths to match honestly (not copy):**
   - Visible, quantified social proof (once verified — A4–A7, C1–C3)
   - Third-party rating surfaces (Clutch/Google — C1/C2)
   - Company timeline storytelling (B10)
   - FAQPage schema everywhere relevant (Savo has it on key pages;
     extend to all service/industry pages — noted for Stage 4)
3. **Competitor patterns to avoid (validated the spec's instincts):**
   - Carousel-heavy pages (176/141 refs) hurting performance
   - Superlative title stacking ("Top... USA, India & UAE")
   - Unverified large counters (4500+, 1200+) — precisely what Part 117
     forbids
   - Near-zero security/ownership communication — Savo's trust center
     is a genuine differentiator to lead with
4. **Strategic takeaway:** Savo should compete on evidence + honesty +
   engineering depth, exactly as the master spec directs. The two
   competitors are beatable on every dimension the spec prioritizes —
   the only race is supplying real proof data.
